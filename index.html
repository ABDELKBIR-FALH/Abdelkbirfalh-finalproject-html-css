What is Economic Abuse?

Economic abuse occurs when an intimate partner or family member systematically controls a woman's ability to acquire, use, and maintain money, property, or employment. Unlike physical violence, it leaves no visible bruises—yet it strips away freedom, safety, and the practical means to leave an unhealthy environment.

Invisible Barrier

Lack of independent funds is the #1 reason women cannot safely leave abusive homes.

Recognized Human Rights Issue

Increasingly protected under domestic violence legislation worldwide.
