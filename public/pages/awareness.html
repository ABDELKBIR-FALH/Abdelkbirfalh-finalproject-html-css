8 Forms of Economic Abuse

Economic abuse takes many shapes. Review each pattern below along with concrete real-world examples.

Form 01

Financial Control

Dictating how every dollar is earned, saved, or spent while denying access to shared bank accounts.

Real-world example: A partner confiscates paychecks upon deposit, refuses to share online banking passwords, or demands itemized receipts for every grocery item.

Form 02

Denial of Resources

Withholding essential funds needed for food, healthcare, medication, clothing, or childcare.

Real-world example: Refusing to pay for a woman's prescription medication or children's winter coats despite having sufficient household income.
